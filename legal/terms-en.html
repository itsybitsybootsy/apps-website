3.1 · Skinmetrics

Skinmetrics photographs your skin, has the image described by an AI service and helps you document progress over time: skin score, daily check-in, before-and-after, spot tracking, care routine and reminders. Face detection and alignment when taking photos run locally on your device.

Skinmetrics is not a medical device

We say this in the App and we say it clearly once more here:

Skinmetrics is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The AI analysis is for information only and is not a medical diagnosis. For medical questions, please consult a dermatologist or a physician.

Specifically, this means:

  • Skinmetrics does not detect skin cancer, melanoma or any other condition, and is neither built nor suited for that.
  • The intended purpose of Skinmetrics is expressly wellness, cosmetics and self-observation — not the detection, prevention, monitoring, prediction, prognosis, treatment or alleviation of disease within the meaning of Art. 2 point 1 Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR). Under Recital 19 MDR, software for lifestyle and well-being purposes is expressly not a medical device.
  • Skinmetrics names no medical conditions, issues no findings and gives no treatment recommendations.
  • A low score, an unremarkable result or a “green” result is not an all-clear. Never rely on it if something worries you.
  • Make no treatment decision on the basis of Skinmetrics — do not stop medication, do not start treatment and do not postpone a doctor’s appointment.

Please seek medical help without delay if a mole changes, grows, itches, bleeds, weeps, has blurred borders or is multicoloured, if a wound does not heal, or if you are worried for any reason. This App is not responsible for that.

Where AI is involved

  • Skinmetrics: skin analysis and text evaluation.

You are talking to a machine

The analysis texts in Skinmetrics are machine-generated and may be incorrect. We label this in the App at the relevant place.

AI on this website

As the operator of this website, the transparency obligations of Art. 50 AI Act apply to us insofar as we provide AI-generated content:

  • No AI chatbot on this website — no interaction with an AI system when visiting the site (para. 1).
  • App screenshots are real simulator captures, not AI images.
  • In more detail: Legal Notice (Impressum), AI transparency.

Legal background: The AI Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) requires in Art. 50 para. 1 that people be informed when they interact with an AI system, and in Art. 50 para. 2 that AI-generated content be marked in a machine-readable way and be recognisable as artificially generated. These transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026; the amending regulation Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (“Digital Omnibus”, OJ of 24.7.2026) has postponed nothing in this respect. For the machine-readable marking under para. 2, a transitional period until 2 December 2026 applies to systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026.

We are the provider of these AI systems within the meaning of the Regulation, because we make the apps available under our own name in the App Store — even though the underlying models come from third parties. The labelling therefore takes place in the App at the relevant place and not merely here in the legal text.

In our assessment, our apps are not high-risk AI systems: They are not medical devices (and therefore do not trigger the product route of Art. 6 para. 1) and do not fall under any of the areas of use listed in Annex III. In particular, we do not derive sensitive or protected characteristics such as origin, health status or the like from images.

AI can be wrong

Language and image models produce plausible-sounding answers. They can be factually wrong, incomplete, outdated or made up — even when they sound very confident. The same inputs can produce different results. Always check important statements against a reliable source. We do not owe you any particular analysis result or hit rate.

Processing by service providers

For each AI analysis, Skinmetrics sends your inputs — photos and selected details from your profile — to an intermediary service of Q Systems (Cloudflare Worker), which forwards them via OpenRouter to hosted models (Meta Llama 4, Alibaba Qwen3-VL). This means this content leaves your device and is processed by third-party providers, including outside the EU (USA). The App uses Apple App Attest for this; this generates a pseudonymous device identifier.

Answers you receive in the analysis are AI estimates and may be inaccurate — for example in difficult lighting or depending on skin tone. Please do not enter anything into AI features that should not be processed — no personal data of others, no secrets. Information on pregnancy and allergies is sent along if you have provided it, so that the evaluation fits; answers on skin cancer history and chronic conditions are not sent. What exactly is transmitted is set out in our Privacy Policy.

The AI features require your separate, express consent in the App, which you can withdraw at any time under Settings → Privacy. Without consent, the AI analysis is not available.
